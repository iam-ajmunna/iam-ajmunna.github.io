🧪 Deep Learning Research

MiST-ER: Micro-emotion Selective Temporal Emotion Recognition

A lightweight multimodal AI pipeline combining Audio, Video, and Text streams for micro-emotion classification — one of the hardest open problems in affective computing.

The core challenge: genuine micro-expressions last 40–200 ms and are suppressed by social context, causing most systems to classify noise rather than emotion. MiST-ER isolates these frames using a custom cross-modal attention mechanism and bidirectional fusion layer, achieving 54% accuracy on the MESC benchmark while maintaining edge deployment viability.

PyTorch Multimodal AI Transformers Temporal Attention Edge Deployment NLP
GitHub
Architecture Highlights
  • Custom cross-modal attention across Audio, Video & Text modalities simultaneously
  • Bidirectional temporal fusion layer to isolate emotionally relevant frames and suppress noise
  • Optimised for resource-constrained edge deployment — minimal latency overhead vs accuracy
  • Benchmarked on MESC dataset — the standard for micro-expression recognition

Hybrid CNN–MobileViT for Kidney Stone Detection in CT Scans

A hybrid student model combining MobileViT-Tiny and a CNN, trained via Knowledge Distillation from a ConvNeXt-Tiny teacher. The result: a model 18.5× smaller that retains 99.80% diagnostic accuracy on renal calculi CT imaging.

Trained in just 9 minutes on an NVIDIA T4 GPU — proving that cutting-edge medical AI doesn't require cutting-edge compute. Currently under peer review at SPICSCON 2026.
